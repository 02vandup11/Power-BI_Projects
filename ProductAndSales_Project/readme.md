# 📊 Product and Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 🧠 Overview
The **Product and Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI report designed to visualize and analyze key metrics related to product performance, sales trends, profit, and discounts.  
It provides a clear, interactive summary for tracking business growth and identifying top-performing products, categories, and regions.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics Displayed

| Metric | Value |
|--------|--------|
| **Total Sales** | ₹8,341.22M |
| **Total Units Sold** | 36,900K |
| **Total Profit** | ₹4,788.82M |
| **Total Discount** | ₹126.71M |
| **No. of Products** | 1,690 |
| **No. of Categories** | 8 |
| **No. of Sub-Categories** | 1,690 |

These KPIs summarize overall company performance and serve as the foundation for deeper analysis throughout the dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1. **Top 5 Products by Sales**
- Displays the five highest-selling products by revenue.  
- Products like **Proseware Projector** and **Contoso Projector** appear among the top contributors to total sales.

---

### 2. **Sales by Product Category**
- Pie chart showing category-wise contribution to total sales.  
- Key categories include:
  - **Computers**
  - **Cameras and Video**
  - **Cell Phones**
  - **Music, Movies**
  - **Audio**
- “Computers” lead with the highest share (≈38.48%).

---

### 3. **Monthly Sales Trend**
- Line chart illustrating total sales performance across months.  
- **December** achieved the highest sales at **₹785.82M**,  
  while **January** recorded the lowest at **₹561.22M**.  
- Overall, December’s sales were **40.04% higher** than January’s.

---

### 4. **Sales by Manufacturer**
- Bar chart ranking manufacturers based on sales volume.  
- Top manufacturers include:
  - **Fabrikam, Inc.**
  - **Contoso, Ltd.**
  - **Adventure Works**
  - **Proseware, Inc.**

---

### 5. **Profit by Country**
- World map visualization showing country-wise profit distribution.  
- Larger bubbles indicate higher profitability across regions such as **North America**, **Europe**, and **Asia**.

---

### 6. **Ask a Question (Q&A Feature)**
- Allows users to explore data insights using natural language queries.  
- Example: Asking for “Product categories of product subcategories and total profit” provides instant visual responses.

---

### 7. **Sales by Product Category (Key Influencers)**
- Displays insights on what factors most influence total sales.  
- Highlights which **manufacturer** or **continent** contributes more to total sales.

---

### 8. **Summary Panel**
- Provides automated insights summarizing sales highlights:
  - **December** had the highest total sales (₹785.82M).
  - **December’s sales were 40.04% higher than January.**
  - December contributed **9.42%** of total sales.
  - Total sales across all products ranged from **₹4.47M to ₹5.19M**.

---

## 🧰 Tools and Techniques
- **Tool Used:** Power BI  
- **Visual Types:** Card visuals, bar charts, line charts, pie charts, map visuals, and key influencer visuals.  
- **Features Utilized:**  
  - Filters and slicers (Category, Sub-Category, Country)  
  - Q&A natural language queries  
  - Dynamic KPIs and summaries  

---

## 💡 Insights
- December was the **peak sales month**, contributing the highest revenue.  
- **Computers** and **Cameras** dominate the product categories.  
- **Fabrikam, Inc.** and **Contoso, Ltd.** are the leading manufacturers.  
- Profits are concentrated in **North America** and **Europe**.  
- Discounts remain moderate relative to total sales and profit.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This Power BI dashboard provides a **comprehensive and interactive overview of product and sales performance**, helping stakeholders to:
- Track overall profitability,
- Identify top-selling products and manufacturers,
- Understand sales patterns over time,
- Analyze profit distribution across global regions.
