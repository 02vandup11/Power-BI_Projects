# 📊 Sales Report Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📝 Overview
This Power BI project presents an **interactive Sales Report Dashboard** that provides insights into revenue, profit, and sales performance across multiple categories, products, and time periods.  
It allows users to filter data by **Country**, **Year**, **Month**, and **Quarter**, helping to make informed business decisions through visually rich analytics.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- To analyze total **Revenue**, **Profit**, and **Units Sold**.
- To identify top-performing **Products** and **Sub-Categories**.
- To visualize **Revenue by Category** and **Sub-Category**.
- To track **Gross Profit trends** by **Quarter** and **Month**.
- To assist management in understanding sales distribution across regions and time.

---

## 🧩 Key Insights
- **Total Revenue:** $126.01M  
- **Total Profit:** $86.89M  
- **Total Units Sold:** 4M  
- Highest revenue contribution comes from **“Extra” Sub-Category**.  
- **Quarter 3** recorded the highest **Gross Profit**.  
- **Special Category** dominates overall sales performance.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Dynamic Filters:** Country, Year, Month, and Quarter  
- **KPIs:** Revenue, Profit, and Units  
- **Visuals:**
  - Pie Chart – *Revenue by Sub-Category*
  - Donut Chart – *Revenue by Category*
  - Bar Chart – *Revenue by Product & Sub-Category*
  - Waterfall Chart – *Revenue Contribution Breakdown*
  - Line Charts – *Gross Profit by Quarter and Month*
